What HALO does

Identity. Memory. Relationships.

Every folder you create tells a story. HALO helps you read it.

🎨
Smart Identity
HALO automatically assigns icons, colors, and categories to your folders. 250+ icons. 40 colors. 20 gradients. You can always override.
🧠
Folder Memory
Add a description, track file counts, see last activity. Open a folder after 8 months and instantly know what it contains.
🔗
Relationships
HALO connects related folders automatically. SATURN links to NOVASEC. MBA links to Finance. You can add or remove any relationship.
🔍
Search Inside Files
Not just filenames. HALO indexes PDFs, Word docs, PowerPoints, spreadsheets, and code. Find knowledge, not just files.
⚡
Instant & Local
Everything runs on your device. No cloud processing. No uploads. Results appear in milliseconds from a local index.
🛡️
Private by Design
No account required. No telemetry. No internet needed after install. Your data never leaves your computer. Ever.
