✦ FAQ

Questions people ask

Does HALO upload my files anywhere?
No. Everything runs on your machine. Your files never touch a server, API, or cloud service of any kind. The index lives at ~/Library/Application Support/HALO/ and stays there.
Does HALO require an internet connection?
No. After installation, HALO works fully offline. No telemetry, no analytics, no account required. Not even for updates.
How much disk space does indexing use?
Typically under 100 MB for a few hundred files. The SQLite database is compact and you can vacuum it anytime from the Index menu.
Can I remove indexed folders?
Yes. Remove a folder from HALO anytime — your actual files stay completely untouched. HALO only removes its own index entries.
How is this different from Spotlight?
Spotlight finds filenames and some metadata. HALO searches inside DOCX, PPTX and XLSX files — which Spotlight can't do. HALO also gives folders identity, tracks relationships, and lets you search from a floating overlay with ⌥ Space.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free supports up to 3 folders and PDF/text search. Pro is €9 lifetime — unlimited folders, all document formats (DOCX, PPTX, XLSX), the global overlay, auto-indexing, and all future updates including Windows and OCR.
Is there a subscription?
No. Pro is a one-time €9 payment. No recurring fees. No account. License delivered by email.
